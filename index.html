01 / About

Engineering intelligence
for physical systems.

I am a PhD candidate in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Zaragoza, working with the Applied Mechanics and Bioengineering group at the Aragón Institute of Engineering Research.

My research combines physical knowledge, mathematical structure and modern AI to create simulation models that are accurate, efficient and useful in practice.

A central motivation is enabling personalised, real-time digital twins for biomedical understanding and decision-making.
